Delete your Piknik account

Request permanent deletion of your account and associated data

You can request deletion of your Piknik account without reinstalling the app. Use either path below. We may ask you to verify that you own the account before completing the request.

Request deletion by email

Email support@pknk.app from the address used for your Piknik account (or clearly identify that account), and ask us to delete your account and associated data.

Email a deletion request

Request deletion in the app

Open Piknik → You → Settings → Delete account. Confirm the prompt to start a deletion request email from your device.

What gets deleted

When we complete a verified deletion request, we delete your account and associated personal data we no longer need to keep, including profile information and content tied to your account where feasible. Data we must retain for legal, security, or abuse-prevention reasons will be handled as described in our Privacy Policy.
