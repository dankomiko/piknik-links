Child Safety Standards

Piknik does not allow child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) material, including sexual content involving minors, grooming, or attempts to share or solicit such material. This page describes how we prevent, detect, and respond.

Standards

  • Users must be old enough for the storefront region (generally 13+, with higher limits where required for open discovery).
  • Zero tolerance for CSAE. Accounts involved are banned and content is removed.
  • In-app Report and Block are available on public Pikniks; reports email the safety contact below.
  • Automated screening runs on sealed photos when moderation providers are configured.
  • We cooperate with lawful requests from authorities and use national reporting channels where required.

How to report

In the app: open the Piknik → safety menu (⋯) → Report Piknik. Or email support@pknk.app with “Child safety” in the subject, the Piknik name or link, and what you saw. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

What we do after a report

  1. Review the report and related content as soon as practical (target: within 24 hours for CSAE reports).
  2. Hide the Piknik from Discover and/or remove contributions when warranted.
  3. Suspend or delete accounts that violate these standards.
  4. Preserve evidence when required for law enforcement or mandated reporting.
  5. Notify the reporter when we can do so without compromising an investigation.
