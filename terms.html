1. What Piknik does

Piknik is a social photo and video-sharing experience. A person starts a Piknik, other people contribute their own shots, and the shots are designed to stay hidden until the Piknik is full. When it fills, the people who added a shot can open the reveal.

Depending on the settings the creator chooses, a Piknik may be invitation-based (an Invite Piknik), shared by link or by name, or open for anyone to join, including through Discover (an Open Piknik). The creator may also choose how many people can take part and other available options.

Pikniks do not always fill, unlock, or result in a reveal. A Piknik that stops receiving shots may close before it fills, and content may become unavailable under the rules shown in the app. We do not guarantee that any particular Piknik will be completed, that other people will participate, or that content will remain available for a particular period.
